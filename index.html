Privacy for Press Pulse Log

Last updated 10 October 2026.

The developer of Press Pulse Log is responsible for this page. Questions go to stoyvind@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Sign-in and the log

Press Pulse Log is for adults. Before the log opens you confirm that you are 18 or older, and you agree that the log is health data. There is no password. You sign in with an email code.

After you agree, Supabase stores that email address and the log: doses, phases, the calendar, GKI, pulse sessions, lab results, and colors. That includes blood tests, medicines, chemo, and radiation. The same email on another phone opens the same log. Supabase keeps this data in Ireland. The log is stored because you agreed. The email is stored so the log stays with that address. You can change the entries in the app. Supabase describes its own handling in the Supabase privacy policy.

The one-time code is emailed through Resend, which receives the address only to deliver that message. Resend describes its own handling in the Resend privacy policy.

The app does not track you and has no ads. Signing out, or deleting the app, removes the session from the phone and leaves the log at Supabase.

What you can do in Settings

You can download a file of the log you can see, including the sign-in email.

You can delete your data. That removes the sign-in email and the agreement. A log you own is deleted for everyone in it. A log you only joined stays for the others.

If you own the log, you can delete the log and keep the sign-in. That deletes the log for you and anyone you invited.

If you only joined, you can leave the log. It stays for the others, and your sign-in stays.

Deleting removes the rows from the live database. Supabase can keep a backup copy until that backup expires. The log otherwise stays until you delete it.

A shared log

The owner can name another person's email. Supabase stores that address and a code for 24 hours. The code is shown in the app and is not emailed. The other person signs in with that same email and the code, and then sees the whole log, including blood tests, medicines, chemo, radiation, and GKI. Only people in that log can read it. A newer unused code for the same email replaces the previous one. When the person joins, that invite row is removed. An expired code is removed the next time a code is created.

On this phone

Language, appearance, and zoom stay on this phone. The sign-in session and the optional xAI key stay in the keychain on the phone. The xAI key is not part of the log. A photo or a PDF of a lab list is read on the phone. The camera is used only to take that photo. The picture itself is not sent on.

Feedback opens the Mail app. What you send is the note you write, plus the app version, the iOS version, and the language. The log is not attached.

Lab reports and xAI

The text of a report is sent to xAI at api.x.ai, outside the EEA, only after you confirm that send. It can contain a name or an ID number. The rest of the log is not sent. Nothing from a reading is stored until you approve the draft.

xAI describes its own handling in the xAI privacy policy.

Not medical advice

The app does not give medical advice. It is a personal log.
