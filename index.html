Privacy for Press Pulse Log

Last updated 10 October 2026.

The developer of Press Pulse Log is responsible for this page. Questions go to stoyvind@gmail.com.

Sign-in and the log

Press Pulse Log asks you to sign in with an email code before the log opens. There is no password. Supabase stores that email address and the log: doses, phases, the calendar, GKI, pulse sessions, lab results, and colors. The same email on another phone opens the same log. Supabase keeps this data in Ireland. Supabase describes its own handling in the Supabase privacy policy.

The one-time code is emailed through Resend, which receives the address only to deliver that message. Resend describes its own handling in the Resend privacy policy.

The app does not track you and has no ads. Signing out, or deleting the app, removes the session from the phone and leaves the log at Supabase. The owner can delete the log in Settings. That removes the log for everyone in it. The email address used to sign in stays with Supabase. Write to the address above to ask for that account to be removed.

A shared log

The owner can name another person's email. Supabase stores that address and a code for 24 hours. The code is shown in the app and is not emailed. The other person signs in with that same email and the code, and then sees the same log. Only people in that log can read it. A newer unused code for the same email replaces the previous one.

On this phone

Language, appearance, and zoom stay on this phone. The sign-in session and the optional xAI key stay in the keychain on the phone. The xAI key is not part of the log. A photo or a PDF of a lab list is read on the phone. The camera is used only to take that photo. The picture itself is not sent on.

Feedback opens the Mail app. What you send is the note you write, plus the app version, the iOS version, and the language. The log is not attached.

Lab reports and xAI

The text of a report is sent to xAI at api.x.ai only when you ask to interpret it with AI, or when the rules on the phone find too little and a key is already saved. The rest of the log is not sent. Nothing from a reading is stored until you approve the draft.

xAI describes its own handling in the xAI privacy policy.

Not medical advice

The app does not give medical advice. It is a personal log.
