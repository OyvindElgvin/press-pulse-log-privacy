Privacy for Press Pulse Log

Last updated 8 October 2026.

The developer of Press Pulse Log is responsible for this page. Questions go to stoyvind@gmail.com.

The log stays on the phone

Press Pulse Log is a personal log on the iPhone. Doses, phases, the calendar, GKI, pulse sessions, lab results, and colors stay on the device until you sign in and share the log. Language, appearance, and zoom are stored only on this phone.

The app does not track you and has no ads. Deleting the app deletes the log from the phone. A backup of the phone can still contain the log.

A shared log

You can sign in with an email code and share the log with a household. The shared log and the email address are stored by Supabase in the EU (Ireland), so people you invite can open the same log. An invite stores the invited email address and a code for 24 hours. Only members of that household can read the shared log. Signing out leaves the shared log at Supabase, and the phone shows the log stored on the device. Deleting the app does not delete a shared log. Supabase describes its own handling in the Supabase privacy policy.

Lab reports and xAI

A report is sent to xAI at api.x.ai only when you ask to interpret it with AI, or when the rules on the phone find too little and a key is already saved. The rest of the log is not sent. Nothing from a reading is stored until you approve the draft.

The xAI key stays in the keychain on the phone. It is not part of the log. xAI describes its own handling in the xAI privacy policy.

Not medical advice

The app does not give medical advice. It is a personal log.
