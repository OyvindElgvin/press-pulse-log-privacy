1. Who provides the app

Press Pulse Log is currently run by Øyvind Elgvin as a private individual in Norway. He provides the app and is responsible for these terms. Questions go to support@presspulselog.com.

2. Who the app is for

Press Pulse Log is for adults. Before the log opens you confirm that you are 18 or older, and you agree that the log is health data.

There is no password. You first sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. After that, you can add a passkey. Sign-in is described in the privacy policy.

The app is a personal log of medicines and supplements, doses, health measurements, and blood tests. Health measurements include GKI measurements and pulse sessions. What you can add without the monthly subscription is set out below.

3. Not medical advice

The app does not give medical advice. It is a personal log. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat.

A photo of your blood test results is taken with the camera. The app does not import a photo from the photo library, and it does not import a PDF. The photo is sent only for AI value extraction, and only if you agree. The photo is never stored. Nothing is saved to the log until you approve the draft. The extracted values can be wrong. They do not replace a doctor. That photo is described in the privacy policy.

4. Your log

Supabase is the database. Medicines and supplements, doses, phases, calendar appointments, GKI measurements, pulse sessions, and lab results are end-to-end encrypted on the phone before they are sent. The decryption key is created on the phone. It is kept in iCloud Keychain and in the QR share code. The decryption key is never sent to the database. If the key is lost, the data may not be recoverable. No backup copy is kept.

What is stored, and what is not, is described in the privacy policy.

5. Sharing a log

The person who created the log can share it with a QR code or a text code. Anyone with the code can read the log and cannot change it. You are responsible for who receives the code. A person who only reads a shared log does not pay. Who can see a log is described in the privacy policy.

6. The monthly subscription

Press Pulse Log is free to download. Dose logging and the dose chart are free to use.

The calendar, GKI measurements, pulse sessions, blood tests, and the share code need the monthly auto-renewing subscription. The subscription is oyvineng.presspulselog.monthly. The length is one month. The price is the price shown in the App Store.

Payment, renewal, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple in the Apple ID account settings. The app checks subscription status through RevenueCat. The privacy policy describes that check. The app does not receive your card or payment details. Restore Purchases is in Settings. Cancelling does not delete the log. Deleting your data does not cancel the Apple subscription.

  • Payment is charged to the Apple ID account when the purchase is confirmed.
  • The subscription renews unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • The account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price shown for the renewal.
  • You manage the subscription and turn off renewal in the Apple ID account settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple.
